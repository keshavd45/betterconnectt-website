Everything a tutor needs.
Nothing they don't.

One clear action per screen. No jargon. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use BetterConnect in 5 minutes.

Batch management
Create batches for each class — set subject, grade, board, timing and fee. Add students with parent details in seconds. Sorted by next upcoming class automatically.
Attendance in seconds
Tap "All present", then mark only the absent students. Parents are notified via app automatically. Monthly totals entry available for catch-up.
Fee tracking & reminders
See who's paid, who's due and who's overdue at a glance. Reminders go out automatically on the due date and 3, 5 and 7 days after — no awkward conversations needed.
Test reports in 60 seconds
Enter marks for all students, publish — ranks, percentages and class average are calculated instantly. Reports sent to every parent via app.
Parent & student app
Every tutor brings their students onto the app. Parents see fees, attendance, test results and announcements in one place. No separate setup needed.
Announcements
Holiday? Class cancelled? Send a notice to one batch or all batches in one tap. All students and parents receive it instantly via app notification.
